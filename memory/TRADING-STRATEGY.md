# Trading Strategy

## Mission
Beat SPY over any rolling 12-month window. Hold quality businesses through volatility. Be patient on entries, opportunistic on dislocations, and slow to exit winners.

## Style
Long-term / opportunistic position investing. Hold periods: months to years. This is NOT swing trading. A week with no trades is a good week if the market hasn't presented a compelling entry.

---

## Entry Criteria (all must pass before any buy)

**1. Business quality (via FMP MCP):**
- ROE > 15%
- Net margin > 10%
- Debt/Equity < 1.5x (non-financials); for financials, use price/book and ROE instead
- Positive and growing free cash flow

**2. Valuation:**
- P/E reasonable for sector and growth rate (PEG < 2 preferred)
- P/B not stretched relative to the stock's own 5-year history
- Forward P/E shows growth is priced in but not wildly speculative

**3. Entry timing (why now, not just why ever):**
- Stock is trading at or near a multi-year low P/E, OR
- Post-earnings panic with no fundamental deterioration, OR
- Sector-wide selloff dragging a quality name down with it, OR
- Specific dislocation event (macro fear, index rebalance, etc.)
- "Good company" alone is not enough — need a reason the price is attractive today

**4. Position limits:**
- Max 15% of portfolio equity per position
- Max 12 open positions total
- Available cash must cover the buy without going below 10% cash reserve

---

## Conviction Tiers

**HIGH** — Telegram alert fires immediately, market-open routine executes:
- All quality gates above pass, AND
- Valuation at or below the stock's own 3-year low P/E, AND
- Clear dislocation reason documented
- Target: ≤ 2 high-conviction alerts per month. If firing more often, criteria are too loose.

**MEDIUM** — Logged in research, surfaced in weekly review:
- Quality passes, valuation fair but not cheap
- Worth accumulating context; wait for a better entry

**LOW / WATCH** — Watchlist only, no Telegram:
- Good business but fairly or richly valued
- Monitor for deterioration or a better entry window

---

## Scaling In
- Adding to an existing position on a dip > 7% from last buy price is allowed
- Fundamentals must be unchanged
- Position must remain ≤ 15% of equity after the add

---

## Exit Rules

**Sell immediately (thesis break):**
- Business deterioration: declining ROE/margins for 2+ consecutive quarters with no recovery thesis
- Fraud or material misrepresentation
- Major guidance cut (> 20% EPS revision) with no one-time explanation
- Regulatory action that kills the core business model

**Sell at weekly review (hard stop):**
- Position down > 15% from cost basis with no thesis-break event — evaluate at weekly review, not midday
- Exception: if down > 15% AND a clear thesis-break event occurred intraday, close immediately

**Consider trimming (all three must be true simultaneously):**
1. Valuation has stretched to 2x or more the stock's own 5-year average P/E
2. Revenue or earnings growth is visibly decelerating (not a single bad quarter — a trend)
3. A clearly cheaper, equally high-quality alternative exists in the watchlist right now

If you cannot name the specific stock you would swap into, do not trim.

**Do NOT sell because:**
- Stock is up 20%, 30%, 50% — price appreciation is not a sell signal for a quality business
- Market is having a bad week
- Sector is rotating out of favour
- Short-term sentiment is negative

---

## Position Management
- No trailing stops as GTC orders — they shake out long-term positions on noise
- Hard stop levels (-15% from cost basis) are evaluated at weekly review
- Thesis reviewed monthly; price vs fair value estimate reviewed weekly

---

## Benchmarks
- Minimum floor: beat SGD fixed deposit rate (~3-4% annually)
- Primary benchmark: beat SPY (S&P 500 ETF) over any 12-month rolling window
- Weekly tracking: note SPY weekly return in every weekly review
